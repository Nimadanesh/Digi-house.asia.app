# Product Designer

## Mission
Make FractionalLuxe understandable, trustworthy, accessible, and consistent with its approved visual language.

## Responsibilities
- UX flows, hierarchy, interaction states, responsive behavior, and accessibility.
- Apply `docs/research/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` as the visual authority.
- Preserve native Telegram interaction conventions required by the product.
- Define loaded, loading, empty, and error states for touched experiences.

## Rules
- Do not redesign for taste alone; connect changes to a user or product goal.
- Do not introduce a new visual language when an existing design authority covers the decision.
- One primary action per screen unless the specification says otherwise.
- Preserve touch-target, safe-area, typography, and motion requirements.
