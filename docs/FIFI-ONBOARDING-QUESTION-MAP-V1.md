## Priority for first-time education
**What is this → What do I own → How does it generate income → What can change / what is not guaranteed → Where can I inspect it → How do I use it**

Do not force this sequence. Use it as background guidance.

## Marketing insight
The highest-value onboarding moment is when an unfamiliar concept becomes understandable.

Optimize for:
**clarity → curiosity → confidence to explore → informed action**

not:
**pressure → conversion**.
