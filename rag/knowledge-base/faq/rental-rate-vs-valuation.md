# What is the difference between rental rate and valuation?

**Q:** What is the difference between rental rate and valuation?

**A:** They answer two different questions:

- **Rental rate / ANR:** How much is the villa listed for per night? ANR is the mean of the villa's listed **full-buyout** nightly rates and is used as the rental-income modeling basis.
- **Valuation:** What is the whole villa estimated to be worth? The product valuation is a conservative estimate based on regional comparable properties and the approved valuation methodology.

### Why the distinction matters

The rental rate is an input to **income modeling**.

The valuation is used in the **share structure and valuation/growth view**.

For example, purely as an illustration, a villa could have a modeled ANR of $10,000/night while its estimated whole-villa valuation is $20 million. Those numbers are not supposed to be close: one describes a rental price, the other describes an asset value.

Also, **ANR is not ADR**. ADR would require actual sold-night revenue and occupancy data; occupancy is currently UNKNOWN across the 24 canonical estates.

**Rule:** never use the rental rate as the villa's value, and never use the valuation as its nightly rental price.
