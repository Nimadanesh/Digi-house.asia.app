# What is the difference between rental rate and valuation?

**Q:** What is the difference between rental rate and valuation?

**A:** The **rental rate (ANR)** is the mean of the villa's listed full-buyout nightly
prices — the basis for modeling rental income. The **valuation** is the product-approved
estimate of what the whole villa is worth (conservative, from regional comparables).
Rate drives income projections; valuation drives share count (valuation ÷ $100) and the
Est. Growth range. Never mix them.
