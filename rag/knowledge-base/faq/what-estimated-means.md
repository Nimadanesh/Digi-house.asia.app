# What does ESTIMATED mean?

**Q:** What does ESTIMATED mean?

**A:** A model- or third-party-derived figure given as a range with its method and
source (e.g. a research valuation leg). It is always quoted as a range — never a
single midpoint, and never as the official figure. The upper end of a valuation
range shown as "Est. Growth" is estimated growth potential: non-annualized, and
never rental income.
