# What is the Club?

**Q:** What is the Club?

**A:** The **FractionalLuxe Club** is the platform's private membership layer. It adds a more exclusive experience around the core product through membership levels, access, priority benefits, and Club-specific experiences.

There are five levels:

- **Standard:** $0+
- **Private:** $10,000+
- **Private+:** $25,000+
- **Elite:** $100,000+
- **Signature:** $500,000+

The Club currently defines benefits such as **Villa Stay, Priority Access, Private Escape, Concierge, Private Club Card, and Referral Rewards**. These benefits do not all have the same operational status: some are product/prototype scope, and stay availability and a user's personal entitlement require live product data.

The important distinction is: **Club membership is not a promise of unlimited stays or investment returns.** Villa access comes from a finite Club pool and is subject to the applicable eligibility, allocation, availability, and booking rules.
