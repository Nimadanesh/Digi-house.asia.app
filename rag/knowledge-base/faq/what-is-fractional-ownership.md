# What is fractional ownership?

**Q:** What is fractional ownership? What do I actually own?

**A:** Instead of buying a whole villa, you own a fraction of it, represented by
shares (1 share = 1/totalShares of the estate position). Your holdings record is
your economic position. The NFT receipt is a display-only collectible — never legal
ownership, a deed, or a title.
