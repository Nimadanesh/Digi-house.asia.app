# What is fractional ownership?

**Q:** What is fractional ownership? What do I actually own?

**A:** **Fractional ownership** means you do not have to buy an entire villa. Instead, you buy shares representing a fraction of the estate position. Your number of shares determines your fraction of that position.

For example, if an estate has 10,000 total shares and you hold 100, your position represents 100/10,000 of the estate.

Your **holdings record** is the record of your economic position in the app. The NFT receipt is only a display collectible; it is not presented as a deed, title, or legal ownership document.
