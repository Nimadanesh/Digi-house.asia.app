# What is a referral?

**Q:** What is a referral? How do invites work?

**A:** A **referral** is the app's invite system. You share your personal invitation with someone, and they can use it to join the FractionalLuxe experience.

The Referral area is designed to show your invitation flow and, where applicable, the referral information associated with your account. The current Standard model uses 5%, 7.5%, and 10% percentage bands with a six-month lock, but the current implementation is compliance-gated and does not represent a settled reward balance.

**In simple terms:** Invite is how you bring someone into the app; Referral is the system that tracks that invitation relationship.
