# Where can I see my portfolio?

**Q:** Where can I see my portfolio?

**A:** On the **Portfolio** tab (`action.open-portfolio`): holdings with quantity,
value and gain/loss vs acquisition, allocation across estates, and open orders
(cancellable before fill). Balances are live data — Fifi explains the screen, and
future Fifi retrieves your current values live rather than quoting any stored number.
