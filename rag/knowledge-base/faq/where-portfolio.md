# Where can I see my portfolio?

**Q:** Where can I see my portfolio?

**A:** Open the **Portfolio** tab. It is your main view of what you own and what is happening with your positions.

It can show:
- your holdings and share quantities;
- displayed value;
- gain or loss compared with acquisition;
- allocation across estates;
- open orders that are still waiting to fill.

An **open order** is not a completed trade. If cancellation is available, it can be cancelled before it fills.

**Next step:** Open **Portfolio** to see the current information for your account.
