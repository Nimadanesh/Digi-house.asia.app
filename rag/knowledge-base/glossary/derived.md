# Derived

**FractionalLuxe meaning:** calculated from stated inputs by the V1 engine
(e.g. ownershipPerShare = 1/totalShares, per-share monthly = owner pool ÷ shares ÷ 12).
Stated with its basis shown; never recomputed ad hoc by Fifi — figures trace to the
engine.

**Related:** Observed, Estimated, V1 engine.
