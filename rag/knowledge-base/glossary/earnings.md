# Earnings

**FractionalLuxe meaning:** what the Earnings screen shows, in three separate
numbers that are never blended: **Received in total** (paid only), **Accrued income**
(earned on locked shares, paid with the next monthly distribution), and **Expected**
(pending distributions). Per-estate paid income is listed under Income by estate.

**Related:** Accrued, Paid, Projected, Withdrawal.
