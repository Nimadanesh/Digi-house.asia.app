# Estate

**FractionalLuxe meaning:** an Estate is the property represented in the app as an investment opportunity. The current marketplace contains 24 canonical luxury villas.

Each Estate has information such as its name, location, property details, rental-rate information, valuation, and share structure.

**In simple terms:** **Villa** describes the physical property; **Estate** is how that property is represented inside FractionalLuxe.

**Related:** Villa, Fractional Ownership, Valuation.
