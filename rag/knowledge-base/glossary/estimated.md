# Estimated

**FractionalLuxe meaning:** a model- or third-party-derived figure within a stated
range, with method and source disclosed. Always quoted as a range — never a bare
midpoint, never presented as an official observed fact. Research valuation legs are
ESTIMATED.

**Related:** Observed, Derived, Valuation.
