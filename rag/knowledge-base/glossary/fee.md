# Fee

**FractionalLuxe meaning:** three separate charges that must never be conflated:
(1) **platform commission** on every buy/sell (9-tier table, `PRODUCT-PLAN.md` §0.5);
(2) **withdrawal 1% fee** at request time (net in 4 weekly installments; legal
classification reserved); (3) **7% primary buyback discount** (not a fee — the
platform's repurchase price during Primary Offering). Exact commission rates are never
quoted from memory.

**Related:** Withdrawal, Commissions & Fees guide.
