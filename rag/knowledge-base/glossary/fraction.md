# Fraction / Share

**FractionalLuxe meaning:** the smallest ownable unit of an estate. Primary price:
**$100 base per share** (approved valuation ÷ 100). Total shares = valuation ÷ $100
(e.g. Grand: $8,000,000 → 80,000 shares). Secondary-market shares trade at
demand-driven prices, separate from the $100 base.

**Related:** Ownership, Primary Offering, Secondary Market.
