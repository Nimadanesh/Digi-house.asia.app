# Fractional Ownership

**FractionalLuxe meaning:** owning a fraction of a luxury villa — represented by
shares — instead of buying the whole property. One share represents `1 / totalShares`
of the estate position. The holdings record is the user's economic position; the NFT
receipt is a display-only collectible, never legal ownership, a deed, or a title.

**Related:** Estate, Ownership, Shares ($100 primary base), Rental Income.
