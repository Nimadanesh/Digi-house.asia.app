# Investment

**FractionalLuxe meaning:** buying one or more shares of an estate (from $100 per
share at the primary offering) to hold an ownership-backed position: participation
in monthly operating income (when locked) plus potential asset-value appreciation.
Projected figures are never promises; Fifi gives no investment advice.

**Related:** Fraction, Rental Income, Valuation, Risk disclosures (Ownership tab).
