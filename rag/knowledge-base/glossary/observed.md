# Observed

**FractionalLuxe meaning:** a directly listed or sourced fact — a name, location,
listed nightly rate, published fee, or check-in time. Safe to state directly, with
source attribution on first use. The strongest everyday provenance in the KB.

**Related:** Estimated, Derived, ANR.
