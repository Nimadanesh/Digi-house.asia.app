# Portfolio

**FractionalLuxe meaning:** the screen (`action.open-portfolio`) showing your holdings
(quantity, value, gain/loss vs acquisition), allocation across estates, and open
orders (cancellable before fill). Balances and holdings are **live data** — the KB
explains what the screen means, never current values.

**Related:** Ownership, Earnings, Open Orders.
