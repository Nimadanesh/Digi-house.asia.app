# Portfolio

**FractionalLuxe meaning:** your Portfolio is the screen that brings your holdings and positions together.

It can show your share quantities, displayed value, gain or loss compared with acquisition, allocation across estates, and open orders.

**In simple terms:** Portfolio answers **“What do I currently hold, and what is the status of those positions?”**

Your personal figures are account information and can change over time.

**Related:** Ownership, Earnings, Open Orders.
