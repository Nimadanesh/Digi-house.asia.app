# Rental Income

**FractionalLuxe meaning:** the villa's hospitality revenue (modeled from ANR-based
scenarios minus V1 cost lines), distributed to locked-share holders as **monthly
profit per share** on the full monthly rate, grounded in the **Average scenario**.
Projected ≠ accrued ≠ paid. Current demo paid entries are labeled "simulated".

**Related:** Earnings, ANR, Average scenario, Locking.
