# Villa

**FractionalLuxe meaning:** the physical luxury property behind an Estate record —
bedrooms, amenities, location, stay rules, and listed nightly/weekly rates as published
by the source listing (Rental Escapes). Per-villa facts live in the generated
`re-*.md` documents; unknown fields stay Unknown.

**Related:** Estate, ANR, Stay rules.
