# Yield

**FractionalLuxe meaning:** an economic attribute of a position — the return relative
to capital over a period — **not the identity of an Estate**. User-facing income is
communicated as **monthly profit per locked share** (Average scenario). "Weekly yield"
and "weekly profit" are banned as product claims.

**Related:** Rental Income, Projected, Locking.
