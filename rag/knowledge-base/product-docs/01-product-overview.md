# Product Overview — What FractionalLuxe Is

## What it is

FractionalLuxe is the **fractional-ownership branch of the parent company Rental Escapes**,
which manages and operates luxury villas. Through FractionalLuxe we sell fractional
ownership (shares) of these villas to users. Rental income generated by the villas is
distributed to shareholders as profit, and the capital raised from selling shares is used
to acquire more villas — growing the portfolio and company revenue.

FractionalLuxe has **its own website and app** (`app.fractionalluxe.com`, marketed via
`fractionalluxe.com`), implemented **across multiple blockchains** so users have broader
access: TON via TonConnect plus EVM chains (Ethereum, BSC, Polygon, Arbitrum) via
wagmi-based wallet connection. It is not a Telegram-only product and not limited to a
single chain.

## Core value proposition

- **Access:** real estate is the most exclusive asset class; FractionalLuxe lets small
  budgets buy a fraction of a single luxury estate instead of being priced out entirely.
- **Monthly profit per locked share:** estates operate as luxury hospitality rentals run by
  Rental Escapes. Rental income is distributed to shareholders as **profit per share,
  calculated and communicated as a MONTHLY amount on the full monthly rate**.
  Projected income is shown in scenarios plus an Average scenario;
  the **Average scenario is the basis for actual payouts**. To receive profit,
  the user **must lock their shares** — unlocked shares earn nothing (this
  prevents earning yield while selling on the secondary market). New locks are
  monthly-only; preserved historical weekly records are labeled Legacy.
- **Withdrawals:** request at any time; a **1% fee** is charged at request time and the
  net is paid in **exactly 4 weekly installments** (a payment schedule, not profit
  frequency).
- **Liquidity for owners:** some villas are owned by the parent company (Rental Escapes)
  — for these we raise liquidity without selling the full villa. Other villas are under
  contractual agreements with owners who allow fractional sales on the platform to raise
  liquidity while keeping operational control.
- **Own platform, multi-chain:** our own website and app, implemented across multiple
  blockchains for broader access — not Telegram-only, not single-chain.
- **Growth flywheel:** capital raised from share sales funds acquisition of more villas,
  increasing the number of villas under management and growing company revenue — the
  main company goal.

## The Estate-first model

FractionalLuxe is organized around the **Estate** — the real hospitality asset:

- The **Estate** is the underlying economic asset (a real luxury villa with photos, name,
  location, and nightly rental price).
- **Hospitality operations** are the value-creation engine (Rental Escapes manages and
  operates each villa as a luxury rental).
- The **Marketplace** is the capital-allocation and liquidity layer where investors
  choose between estates.
- **Fractionalization** is the access mechanism (shares make entry possible).
- **Portfolio growth** is the long-term flywheel: share-sale capital funds more villa
  acquisitions, growing the villas under management and company revenue.

An Estate is not a decorative image attached to a yield percentage — its real property
facts (photos, name, location, nightly rental price) are part of the product.

## How it works (high level)

1. **Browse** the Marketplace: 24 luxury estates, each with its own economics
   (entry price, funding state, projected income per share).
2. **Connect a wallet** on any supported chain when you first transact (TON or EVM).
3. **Buy shares** of an estate at the primary offering ($100 base price per share at
   the primary offering; secondary-market prices are separate and demand-driven).
4. **Lock to earn:** one share represents `1 / totalShares` of the estate position.
   **Lock your shares to receive the monthly profit per share** (Average scenario =
   payout basis). Unlocked shares earn nothing.
5. **Sell:** ownership positions can be resold on the secondary market once unlocked —
   locked shares are not sellable until unlocked.

## Payouts & markets (summary)

- **Monthly** profit per locked share (Average scenario = payout basis). Withdrawals:
  **1% fee at request time, net in 4 weekly installments**.
- **Primary Offering:** fixed share price ($100 base), the platform itself is the seller;
  sell back to the platform at a **7% discount** during this stage. **Secondary Market:**
  supply-and-demand pricing between users once primary shares sell out. The platform
  takes a commission on every buy and sell (tiers: `PRODUCT-PLAN.md` §0.5).
- Financial detail per villa lives across the Property page tabs (Overview, Income,
  Ownership, Earn, Details).

## Demo honesty (current build)

The current build is a company-review prototype: the full product experience is
demonstrated, and trading/payout flows run on a **demo layer clearly labeled
"Demo mode" / "simulated"**. This permits showing the complete loop — it never permits
fabricated data. Property facts, calculations, market states, ownership states, and
income states remain truthful to their configured source; no fake trades, payouts,
partnerships, or performance history are presented as real.
