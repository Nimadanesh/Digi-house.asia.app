# What FractionalLuxe Is

FractionalLuxe lets people participate in luxury villas through **fractional ownership**: instead of buying an entire villa, you can buy shares representing a portion of it.

The product brings together:
- **Luxury estates** — real villas with their own location, rental-rate information, property facts and economics.
- **Marketplace** — discover estates, review them and, where available, buy or trade shares.
- **Earnings tools** — lock eligible shares and follow rental income associated with them.

## Shares

A share is a proportional ownership unit of an estate. The current Primary Offering uses a **$100 base price per share**.

For example, an $8 million estate divided into 80,000 shares gives each share approximately 1/80,000 of the estate.

Primary price, reference value and Secondary Market price are different concepts. Secondary Market prices come from buyers and sellers.

## Rental income

The villas are operated as luxury rentals. The app models rental income using documented rental-rate scenarios.

For new locks, profit is communicated **monthly per locked share**. The **Average scenario** is the basis used for actual payout calculations.

Shares must be locked to participate in rental profit. Unlocked shares do not earn rental profit.

Projected income is an estimate. It is not money already received and is not a guarantee.

## The normal journey

**Discover an estate → review it → buy shares → decide whether to lock eligible shares → follow Portfolio and Earnings.**

After a Primary Offering is complete, an estate can move to the Secondary Market, where users can place buy or sell orders with each other.

## Withdrawals

A withdrawal can be requested at any time. A **1% fee** is charged when the request is made, and the remaining amount is paid in **exactly four weekly installments**.

Those installments describe the withdrawal payment schedule. They do **not** mean rental profit is calculated weekly.

## When information is unavailable

Every estate has its own facts and economics. When a fact is not verified, the app keeps it unknown rather than filling the gap with a plausible number.

## Finding your way around

**Marketplace** helps you discover.

**Estate page** helps you understand one villa.

**Portfolio** helps you see what you own.

**Earn** helps you control locked shares.

**Earnings** helps you follow income.

**Club** provides the membership experience.

**Invite** provides the referral experience.
