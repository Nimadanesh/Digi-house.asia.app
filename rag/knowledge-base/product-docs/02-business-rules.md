# Product Rules Users Should Understand

These are the rules that most directly affect what you see and can do in the app.

## Estates

The current catalog contains **24 estates**. Each estate has its own name, location, photographs, and rental-rate information.

If a fact is not verified, it remains unavailable. An unavailable value does not mean zero.

## Shares and ownership

A share is a proportional ownership unit of an estate. Your number of shares determines the fraction represented by your position.

## Rental income

For the current earning model:

- rental profit is calculated and communicated **monthly**;
- it is calculated per **locked share**;
- the **Average scenario** is the basis for actual payout calculations;
- unlocked shares do not earn rental profit;
- new locks are monthly-only.

Older weekly lock records may appear in historical information. They are labeled **Legacy** and do not describe the current locking option.

## Withdrawal

A withdrawal request has a **1% fee at request time**.

The remaining amount is paid in **exactly four weekly installments**.

The installments are a payment schedule, not weekly rental profit.

## Primary and Secondary Market

During the **Primary Offering**, the platform offers newly issued shares.

During the **Secondary Market**, users trade existing shares through buy and sell orders.

Eligible Primary Offering buybacks use a **7% discount** to the share price. This is a buyback price adjustment, not a transaction fee.

## Fees

Buy and sell transactions can have a platform commission. The current commission depends on the transaction amount, so the current in-app fee information should be used for a specific transaction.

## Three income words

- **Projected** — an estimate of future income.
- **Accrued** — income earned but not yet paid.
- **Paid** — income actually distributed.

These describe different stages and should not be blended.

## What these rules mean in practice

A modeled income figure is not historical income or a guarantee.

An unavailable number should not be treated as zero.

A withdrawal installment is not a weekly profit calculation.
