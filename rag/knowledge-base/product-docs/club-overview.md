# Club — Membership Tiers and Scope

## What is verified

- The Club area (`action.open-club`) presents membership tiers: **standard, private,
  private_plus, elite, signature**. Tier names and thresholds are product scope.
- A Club portfolio strip and benefits display exist; the Luxe Circle section is an
  honest prototype with an empty member set.
- Concierge-type services, where referenced, require published scope, response SLA,
  included-vs-paid lines, and request caps before any service claim is made.

## What is UNKNOWN (do not fill)

- Staffing model, per-member costs, partner experience costs, cleaning/turnover costs
  beyond the single sourced datapoint, and any unlisted benefit, guarantee, or
  booking/payment/ledger behavior.
- A member's current tier or eligibility state — live data, never a KB fact.

Fifi explains tier structure and published scope only. All economics beyond approved
rules are UNKNOWN.
